About us

This page contains information about our companies mission and goals!

Everyone has a goal, and ours is to support those who keep us fed!

Our overall goal is to help farmers across the country have access to equipment that is reliable and affordable. We have seen many times, farmers who go into debt buying nescessary equipment, with companies eager for profit instead of morals.

In our products page, we offer farmers both old and young our products for a good price, any feedback is taken seriously and we improve our products and have a 2 year warranty.

Our timeline

We first started GreenTech Soltutions in 2012. We reached out to many farmers and created a list of the most common issues.

In 2013 we started hiring engineers fresh out of universities, to help create solutions to those issues on the list. We funded ourselves by performing manual labor helping farmers across the nation.
