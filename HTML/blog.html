Eco-Friendly Technology Tips

10/2/15

  1. Optimize Energy Usage: Install, smart thermostats, use smart power strips to eliminate "vampire" energy, and switch to LED lighting to reduce energy consumption by up to 90%.
  2. Upgrade Smartly: Choose devices with, ENERGY STAR or EPEAT certifications.
  3. Reduce Digital Footprint: Clean out, unnecessary emails, store data efficiently, and lower screen brightness to save energy.
  4. Utilize Eco-Apps: Use transportation apps for tracking, public transit or finding bike-shares.
